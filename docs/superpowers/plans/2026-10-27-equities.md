# Equities plan

Spec: [`../specs/2026-10-27-equities-design.md`](../specs/2026-10-27-equities-design.md). Tracked in one
draft PR; each stage is its own commits, and each ends with the regression gate below.

## The regression gate (every stage)

`scripts/equities/regression-gate.sh <old-dist> <new-dist>` backtests a fixed corpus on both builds and
diffs the trade lists byte for byte:
- `examples/` and `strategies/` in this repo that parse and have data;
- the forward book (bot2 `/root/forward-stack`) and the hg20k book on the forge store;
- the measured research strategies (bot2 `/var/tmp/eventbt/reactive`, `strategies/brk_*`);
- the attestation catalog's replay strategies.
Any difference fails the stage unless it is the stage's intended change, listed with its cause.
Plus `./gradlew check` and, for stages touching live paths, the attestation catalog on the Exness demo.

## Standing rules (from review, 2026-10-27)

- **One source of truth per fact.** A venue fact (contract size, category, trade mode, swap) reaches the engine only
  through the connector's instrument contract (`TradingAccount.instrumentSpec` -> `InstrumentMeta`). A connector
  translates its wire fields in one function; calendars, risk and sizing read the generic fields.
- **File limits by extraction.** A change to a file at or over its limit first moves a cohesive area out (move-only,
  proven by the regression gate), lowers the baseline, then lands in the extracted file. No comment squeezing.
- **Duplicates removed when confirmed.** Two copies of one behaviour become one only after the copies are shown
  identical, the suite passes and the gate shows identical trades; a copy that differed is called out as a fix.
- **Every edge a test can reach is tested** at the lowest level that proves it (unit, scenario, backtest e2e), and
  every edge the venue can show is an attestation case or a scheduled soak with recorded evidence.

## Step 1: stock CFDs on Exness

- [x] **1.1 Session windows on calendars.** `nyse[06:00-15:45]` grammar; early close = min(window end,
  exchange early close); DST; parse at the config boundary. Unit tests on both DST switches, every
  holiday kind, early-close days, special closures; resolver tests for config and profile rules.
- [x] **1.2 Category calendar rules.** `category:<glob>` keys; category from the MT5 `path` (live symbol
  cache) and `category:` in `instruments.yaml`; `instruments pull` writes it; Exness profile default
  `category:Stocks -> nyse[06:00-15:45]`; ticker rules override category rules.
- [x] **1.3 Trade-mode guard.** `tradeMode` instrument field from `/symbol_info` and YAML; opening
  orders refused on close-only/disabled (named reason, backtest and live), closing orders allowed.
- [x] **1.4 DAY orders expire at session end** (defect, also FX). `expiresAt` from the symbol calendar;
  MT5 GTD; mt5-sim and paper expire there. Before/after evidence for existing DAY users.
- [x] **1.5 Non-trade deals booked.** Dividend/charge/correction/tax/interest deals as venue costs
  (`CostKind.DIVIDEND`), attributed by position; unknown types counted and logged.
- [x] **1.5b Connector contract and shared setup** (review). One MT5 translation (`MT5SymbolInfo.instrumentMeta`)
  for the live registry and `instruments pull`; one generic category lookup (`InstrumentCategories`) every connector
  binds; `TradeMode` venue-neutral. `BacktestSetup` shared by strategy and portfolio backtests (619 -> 500 lines in
  `BacktestContext`); the portfolio path now provisions `MACRO:` series like the strategy path; the portfolio parity
  fixture runs the production `PortfolioBacktestWiring` instead of a copy.
- [ ] **1.6 Instruments and data.** Found and fixed: tick coverage judged days by qkt's built-in calendars, not the
  run's, so a stock's NYSE holidays read as missing data (`166 of 171`); `qkt fetch` judged stock days on the FX week. `instruments pull` for the stock symbols (contract size 100,
  Friday triple swap, category, trade mode); Exness M1 bars and ticks captured into a data root;
  backtest replays of the captured window.
- [ ] **1.7 Live evidence on the Exness demo** (US session 10:00/11:00-19:45/20:45 UTC):
  attestation lane `stocks` with cases:
  - entry at the 06:00 New York open and exit before 15:45; no evaluation outside the session;
  - market, limit, stop, bracket, timed exit, re-entry on a stock;
  - DAY limit expiring at the session end;
  - opening order refused on a close-only symbol (`FBm`) and a disabled one (`ATVIm`); closing allowed;
  - PCT OF EQUITY / USD / RISK sizing with contract size 100, min 0.01 lot = 1 share;
  - overnight hold: swap booked at the measured rollover, Friday triple;
  - restart over an open stock position;
  - a position held through an ex-dividend date: the dividend deal booked;
  - replay of each live window through ticks, bars and mt5-sim: zero unexplained differences.
  Early-close and holiday behaviour proven by replay on Exness's own 2025 bars (no early close
  falls inside the live window).
- [ ] **1.8 Docs.** How-to for stock CFDs; calendar reference (`[hh:mm-hh:mm]` windows, `category:`);
  market coverage table.

## Step 2: cash equities (Alpaca first)

Each stage lands with unit tests, a fake-venue contract test, the regression gate, and, from 2.3 on,
Alpaca paper evidence.

- [ ] **2.0 Read contract split.** `Broker`'s read-only venue view as its own `VenueReads` interface, so an adapter
  implements a small documented read contract; `AdjustmentSource` beside it.
- [ ] **2.1 Equity product kind and the VGP instrument record.** `WireInstrument` gains `category` and `trade_mode`
  (and `kind: equity`); `GatewayConnector` translates it to `InstrumentMeta` in one function, implements
  `instrumentSpec`, and binds calendars with `withInstrumentCategories` - so a VGP venue adapter only fills wire fields.
- [ ] **2.1b Equity product kind.** `ProductType.EQUITY` in qkt; VGP wire `kind: equity` (spec bump,
  both repos); quote currency from the instrument; compiler-forced handling at every product branch.
- [ ] **2.2 Equity instrument and account model.** Whole/fractional shares, cash vs margin account,
  buying power, settled vs unsettled cash; no CFD financing on equities.
- [ ] **2.3 Alpaca adapter in qkt-venue-gateway.** Auth, assets (tradable, shortable, easy-to-borrow,
  fractionable), orders (market, limit, stop, stop-limit, trailing; DAY, GTC, OPG, CLS, IOC, FOK;
  extended hours), trade updates stream, positions, account, clock and calendar, IEX quotes. Contract
  tests against recorded Alpaca responses; paper account end to end.
- [ ] **2.4 Corporate actions.** Hub dataset (splits, dividends, symbol changes, delistings) with
  `known_at`; one engine boundary applies them: split restates quantity and average price (no P&L),
  dividend books cash on pay date (debit when short), symbol change maps the position; adjusted and
  unadjusted price views for backtests; live reconciliation after the venue applies them. Also
  applied to stock CFDs (cash adjustment) for backtests.
- [ ] **2.5 Sessions and auction orders.** Pre/post-market on the NYSE calendar, extended-hours flag
  on orders, `OPG`/`CLS` TIF (market/limit on open/close), halts (orders held/refused), NASDAQ calendar.
- [ ] **2.6 Account rules.** Buying-power check before submission; short sale needs shortable +
  borrow availability, borrow fee booked as `CostKind.BORROW`; PDT limit under $25k; T+1 settlement
  for cash accounts (no free-riding).
- [ ] **2.7 Costs.** Minimum commission per order, SEC fee on sells, FINRA TAF per share with cap;
  VGP cost kinds for regulatory fees.
- [ ] **2.8 Universes.** Point-in-time symbol lists including delisted names; cross-sectional rank
  over a universe.
- [ ] **2.9 Live evidence on Alpaca paper.** Attestation lane `equities`: every order type and TIF,
  extended hours, auction orders, fractional shares, short sale refused when not shortable, buying
  power refusal, a split and a dividend replayed and reconciled, restart over positions.
- [ ] **2.10 Docs and coverage table.**
