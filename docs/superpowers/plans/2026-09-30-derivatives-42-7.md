# Phase 42.7: futures margin and exchange calendars — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A futures backtest refuses orders the account could not margin and reports its daily margin;
futures roots trade on their exchange's hours (`calendar: cme_globex` for CME products).

**Architecture:** `MarginModel` (accounting/margin) turns a root's `MarginTerms` and a position into
initial and maintenance margin in account currency. A pre-trade `MarginRequirement` rule (risk/rules)
refuses an order that opens or adds exposure when equity less the initial margin of every position
after the order would be negative; it is registered, in `ReplayRisk` and in `LiveSession`, only when a
traded symbol has margin terms. A `MarginDailySampler` writes `margin_daily.csv` rows for futures runs
only. `CmeGlobexCalendar` (common) models America/Chicago Sunday 17:00 – Friday 16:00 with the daily
16:00–17:00 halt out of session; a root's `calendar:` key names its calendar, and a run whose symbols
are all futures uses their common calendar (any CFD keeps today's first-symbol rule).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-30-futures-options-design.md` (§5.8, §5.10, §12)

## Global constraints

- CFD golden pins, DSL corpus pin and report bundle list unchanged; no rule, file or calendar change for runs without futures.
- Files ≤ 200 lines (tests ≤ 220); baselined files only shrink (`ReplayRisk`, `LiveSession`, `BacktestContext`: extract first).
- Money `BigDecimal`; KDoc on public API; commits via the helper.

## Review focus

- A CFD-only run registers no margin rule and resolves the same calendar as before (golden pins).
- An exit is never refused for margin; a flip is judged on the exposure after the order.
- Margin on a continuous stream uses the stream's price (declared divergence: the contract price differs by the roll shift).
- The CME halt: DAY orders expire at 16:00 Chicago; session indicators skip the halt; DST switches keep 17:00 local.
- A root naming an unknown calendar fails at load with the known names.

---

### Task 1: MarginModel
- `MarginModel(instruments, accounting)`: `initial(symbol, quantity, price)`, `maintenance(...)` — `PER_CONTRACT`: `|qty| × amount`; `NOTIONAL`: `|qty| × price × contractSize × rate`; converted to account currency; zero without terms.
- Tests: both bases, maintenance, conversion identity, no terms.

### Task 2: MarginRequirement rule
- `evaluate(request, positions)`: risk-reducing orders pass (existing `isRiskReducing`); otherwise margin of all positions after the order (pending exposure included via the positions provider) against `riskView.currentEquity()`; reject naming required and available margin.
- Registered in `ReplayRisk` and `LiveSession` through one factory `MarginRules.forSymbols(symbols, instruments, …)` returning empty when no traded symbol has margin terms.
- Tests: refuse an unaffordable open, pass an exit and a reducing order, not registered for CFD runs; a backtest on `btcusdt-rolls` with a large notional margin rejects the entry.

### Task 3: margin_daily.csv
- `MarginDailySampler` subscribes in replay (futures runs only) and records, per UTC day end, `date,marginUsed,maintenance,equity,marginCall` (`marginCall` = equity < maintenance). `BacktestResult.marginDaily`; emitted via `FuturesReportFiles` only when non-empty.

### Task 4: CmeGlobexCalendar
- `CmeGlobexCalendar`: `isInSession` false Friday 16:00 – Sunday 17:00 and daily 16:00–17:00 (Chicago); `sessionRange` = the session ending at the next 16:00 Chicago; `anchorEpochFor`/`rangeFor` by session end; `tradingPeriodsPerYear` from 23h × 5 days × 52 weeks. Exchange holidays not modelled (declared).
- Tests: in/out of session around the halt and the weekend, DST change, DAY expiry.

### Task 5: root calendars
- `calendar:` values `crypto | fx | nyse | cme_globex`, validated at root load. `FuturesCalendars.runCalendar(symbols, instruments, default)`: all symbols futures with one common calendar → it; mixed with CFD → `default` (today's first-symbol rule). Wired at the backtest calendar resolution (`BacktestContext`) and the live first-symbol resolution.
- Parity catalog row for exchange holidays; docs for `calendar:` and margin.
