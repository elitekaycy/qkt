# Phase 42.5: futures execution on real contracts — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Orders on a futures stream execute on real contracts. A continuous stream's orders are
translated to the contract the roll schedule names; positions are rolled (carried) at each roll with
the roll's slippage and fees booked as a cost; explicit contracts settle at their delivery price at
expiry. Backtests use an exchange simulator; the same translation layer will wrap the live venue in
phase 44.

**Architecture:** Three new pieces behind the existing `Broker` interface, plus one cost channel.
- `ExchangeSimulator` — a paper exchange for dated contracts: wraps `PaperBroker` on a private bus,
  adds adverse slippage in ticks, a maximum order size, tick-grid validation, netting, expiry
  settlement, and refusal of orders on expired contracts.
- `ContinuousContractBroker` — the single translation boundary between a continuous symbol and its
  contracts (spec §5.5): maps order levels into contract space, republishes the delegate's events in
  continuous space, carries positions and resting orders across rolls, records every roll in a
  `RollLedger`, and refuses new entries on a stream whose roll failed.
- `BrokerEvent.CostIncurred` — a non-execution cost (roll slippage, roll fees) that the pipeline books
  through `NonExecutionAccounting` in both modes.
- `replayBroker` routes continuous symbols to `ContinuousContractBroker(ExchangeSimulator)` and listed
  contracts to `ExchangeSimulator`; every other route keeps today's broker, so CFD runs are unchanged.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-30-futures-options-design.md` (§5.5–5.7, §11 E6, E7, E10–E15)

## Global constraints

- CFD golden pins and the DSL corpus pin stay green.
- Files ≤ 200 lines (tests ≤ 220); baselined files only shrink (extract first).
- Money `BigDecimal`; KDoc on public API; the helper for commits (subject ≤ 70 chars).
- One translation boundary: only `ContinuousContractBroker` converts between contract and continuous
  prices for execution; only `ContinuousMarketSource` does so for data (skill §7).
- Deterministic: no wall clock, no randomness; ids derived from existing ids.

## Rulings carried in

- Spec §5.7 proposed `BrokerKind.EXCHANGE_SIM` and `--broker exchange-sim`. Instead futures routes
  always use the exchange stack, whatever `--broker` says (it keeps choosing the CFD simulator). A
  futures symbol filled by a CFD simulator would be wrong in every case, so the choice is not the
  user's; and no `BrokerKind` `when` site changes. Slippage per fill comes from the futures root's
  `slippageTicks` (mapped onto `InstrumentMeta.slippagePoints`), like CFD `slippagePoints`.
- A failed roll does not call into risk state (a broker cannot); it corrects the engine's position
  with a venue-close fill and the continuous broker then rejects new entries on that stream with a
  reason naming the failed roll. Rejections are recorded and visible in both modes.

## Review focus

- A position held across a roll: the engine's P&L plus the booked roll cost equals the P&L of the
  actual contract legs, to the cent (Task 8 identity test).
- A resting stop/limit on the old contract at the roll: re-placed on the new contract at the same
  continuous level, snapped conservatively; no engine-visible cancel (Task 5 test).
- A roll whose new-contract leg is rejected: the engine's position becomes flat through a venue-close
  fill, later entries on the stream are rejected, CFD streams in the same run are unaffected (Task 6 test).
- An explicit contract held to expiry: closed at the catalog's delivery price, orders after expiry
  rejected (Task 2 test).
- A mixed run (one CFD stream, one futures stream): the CFD stream's fills are byte-identical to a
  run without the futures stream (Task 7 test).

### Task 2: Exchange simulator

**Files:**
- Create: `src/main/kotlin/com/qkt/broker/exchange/ExchangeSimulator.kt`
- Create: `src/main/kotlin/com/qkt/broker/exchange/ExpirySettlement.kt`
- Modify: `src/main/kotlin/com/qkt/instrument/FuturesRoot.kt` + `FuturesRootsFile.kt` (`slippageTicks: Int = 0` → `metaFor(...).slippagePoints`)
- Modify: `src/main/kotlin/com/qkt/execution/ExitReason.kt` (`EXPIRY`, `ROLL_FAILED`)
- Test: `src/test/kotlin/com/qkt/broker/exchange/ExchangeSimulatorTest.kt`, `ExpirySettlementTest.kt`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `PaperBroker`, `InstrumentMeta` (`derivative as FutureTerms`, `pointSize`, `volumeMax`, `slippagePoints`), `ContractCatalog` delivery prices through `FuturesDirectory`.
- Produces: `class ExchangeSimulator(bus: EventBus, clock: Clock, prices: MarketPriceProvider, instruments: InstrumentRegistry, calendar: TradingCalendar) : Broker` with `fun onTick(tick: Tick)`
  - capabilities `MARKET, LIMIT, STOP, STOP_LIMIT`; `positionAccountingMode = NETTING`
  - rejects: quantity above `volumeMax`; a limit/stop price off the tick grid (`price % pointSize != 0`) naming the price; any order on a contract at or after its expiry
  - market fills and triggered-stop fills move `slippagePoints × pointSize` against the order; limit fills are unchanged
  - on the first tick at or after a contract's expiry, every net position the simulator holds in it (per strategy) is closed by an `OrderFilled(updatesOrderExecution = false, exitReason = EXPIRY)` at the delivery price (catalog), or at the last price with a WARN when the catalog has none; working orders on it are cancelled
  - it keeps its own per-strategy net position per contract from its fills (it is the exchange's view)

The simulator never subscribes to ticks itself: whoever owns it calls `onTick` (the replay wiring for
explicit contracts; `ContinuousContractBroker` for continuous streams). `ContinuousMarketSource`
(42.4) appends, for an explicit contract whose run window reaches its expiry, one settlement bar/tick
at `expiryMs` priced at the delivery price with zero volume, so settlement happens even when no other
symbol ticks after expiry — add that to the source in this task, with a test.

- [ ] Step 1: failing tests (slippage direction per side/type; volumeMax; off-grid; expiry settlement
  at delivery price; post-expiry order rejected; no position → no settlement fill).
- [ ] Step 2: implement (PaperBroker on a private `EventBus(clock, MonotonicSequenceGenerator())`;
  subscribe to the private bus's order events, adjust fill prices, republish on `bus`).
- [ ] Step 3: tests + golden pins; commit `feat(broker): add an exchange simulator for dated contracts`.

### Task 5: Continuous contract broker — rolling positions and resting orders

**Files:**
- Create: `src/main/kotlin/com/qkt/broker/continuous/RollExecutor.kt`
- Modify: `ContinuousContractBroker.kt`
- Test: `src/test/kotlin/com/qkt/broker/continuous/RollExecutorTest.kt`

**Rules:**
- A roll happens on the first engine tick whose timestamp maps to a new contract index.
- For each strategy holding the old contract: market close on the old contract at its last price,
  then forward the new contract's tick, then market open on the new contract, same side and quantity.
  Roll order ids are `roll:<stream>:<atMs>:<strategy>:close|open`; their events are never republished.
- Resting orders on the old contract: cancelled on the delegate (cancel event swallowed) and
  re-submitted on the new contract at the same continuous level under venue id `<id>~r<n>`.
- The roll is recorded in the `RollLedger` and its cost published as `CostIncurred` (reason
  `roll <from>-><to>`), using the history's reference prices from the chain (`ContinuousChain.rollPrices(transitionIndex)`, added here).

- [ ] Steps: failing tests (position carried with correct quantity and side; ledger entry; cost event;
  resting stop re-placed at the snapped level; no engine-visible events for roll orders), implement,
  commit `feat(broker): carry futures positions and orders across rolls`.

### Task 6: Roll failure

**Rules:** if the new-contract leg is rejected or fills partially, publish
`OrderFilled(updatesOrderExecution = false, exitReason = ROLL_FAILED)` in continuous space for the
quantity no longer held (so the engine position matches the contracts), log at ERROR, mark the stream
failed for that strategy; subsequent `submit`s that open or increase exposure on it are rejected

**Rules:** partition each route's symbols: continuous symbols (`instruments.futures()?.rootOfContinuous`)

(`BacktestResult.rolls`, used in 42.6). With no futures symbols, the returned broker is built exactly
as today.
